# v0.9.3 — A full disk stops the export, with its files kept

When Chrome can't store the export's files, the export now stops and says so, instead of carrying on and coming out with most of your records missing. And a paused export tells you once, not twice, after Chrome restarts.

> [!NOTE]
> Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Maccabi Healthcare Services.

## What's changed

- **A full disk stops the export.** When Chrome can't store the files it collects, most often because the disk is full, the export stops where it is and the popup says the disk may be full. The files collected so far are kept: free some space, then press **Try again**, and it goes on from the section it stopped in. Before, each record after that point was listed as one that couldn't be exported, and the ZIP came out with most of them missing.
- **One "Export paused" after Chrome restarts.** An export that was running when Chrome closed is paused when Chrome opens again, and the notification that says so now comes once, not twice.
- **A copy of a document is never saved twice.** A document that is the same file as one already in the export is saved once and pointed to, and that now holds even when Chrome stops the extension in the middle of saving one.

Files are named as before, so an export paused on 0.9.2 resumes after the update. The Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use haven't changed.

## Install or update

You need Chrome 120 or newer.

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lmjcbhajlnbpldofejcglcdclceampjp)** and Chrome keeps it up to date, though store review can lag a day or two behind a release here.

To load this ZIP unpacked instead:

1. Download **health-records-export-for-maccabi-0.9.3.zip** from the assets below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder, the one with `manifest.json` in it. Keep that folder: Chrome loads the extension from it.

To update from an earlier version, unzip over the old folder, or load the new one and remove the old entry. Then press the reload arrow on `chrome://extensions`. An export in progress won't survive the swap: finish or stop it first.

Then [export your records](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.9.3/README.md#export-your-records), and [see what to ask your AI assistant](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#ask). The code isn't minified, so you can read exactly what runs.

## Before you start

- **Maccabi will text you.** An export from the popup orders a fresh copy of your medical file, at most once a day, which sends an SMS and replaces the previous file on the site. It's the only change the extension makes to your account.
- **Keep the Maccabi Online tab in front** until the export finishes. Chrome pauses hidden tabs.
- **If your session ends,** log in again and press **Resume**. The files collected so far are kept.

## Privacy

- Talks only to `online.maccabi4u.co.il`. No servers, no analytics, no remote code.
- The extension's links open its website or its GitHub issue tracker in your own browser, and only when you click them. The extension sends neither one anything. The website's home page counts visits without cookies; the extension itself counts nothing.
- Never sees your password or one-time codes. It uses the session you're already logged in with.
- Apart from ordering the medical file, it only reads. Every request it makes is listed in [docs/endpoints/endpoints.json](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.9.3/docs/endpoints/endpoints.json).
- **The extension sends your records nowhere, but an AI service you open them in can read them.** Check what it keeps, and for how long, before you give it your health information.
- **The ZIP isn't encrypted.** It holds your health information: keep it somewhere safe, and take care when you share it.
