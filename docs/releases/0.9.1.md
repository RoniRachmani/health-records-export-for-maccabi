# v0.9.1 — Exports that tidy up after themselves

An export you leave paused is now deleted after a week. A resumed export picks up where it left off inside the two longest sections. And when Maccabi's site changes, the export says so instead of looking empty. What's collected hasn't changed.

> [!NOTE]
> Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Maccabi Healthcare Services.

## What's changed

- **A forgotten export is deleted after 7 days.** A paused or stopped export keeps the files it has collected so far, so it can continue. If it makes no progress for 7 days, the extension now deletes it along with those files, and a notification tells you. You can start a new one whenever you like.
- **Resuming inside the long sections.** Test results and messages from your doctors can take a while. If an export paused partway through one of them, resuming used to go back over every record in that section. Now it skips the ones already collected whole and asks Maccabi only for the rest.
- **A clearer sign when Maccabi's site changes.** If a section comes back in a shape the extension doesn't recognise, it's now reported as a problem ("the site may have changed") in the popup, in `export-errors.json` and in the export's README. Before, that section could look as if you had nothing in it.
- **"1 file", not "1 files"** in the *Export ready* notification.

The export's files and layout are the same as in 0.9.0, so an export paused on 0.9.0 resumes after the update. The [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.9.1/docs/privacy.md) now says that a paused or stopped export is deleted 7 days after it last made progress, and that while a long section is collected, the export's progress keeps short codes made from the names of records already collected. Neither changes what's collected or where it goes, so you won't be asked to accept the policies again. The Terms of Use haven't changed.

## Install or update

You need Chrome 120 or newer.

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lmjcbhajlnbpldofejcglcdclceampjp)** and Chrome keeps it up to date, though store review can lag a day or two behind a release here.

To load this ZIP unpacked instead:

1. Download **health-records-export-for-maccabi-0.9.1.zip** from the assets below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder, the one with `manifest.json` in it. Keep that folder: Chrome loads the extension from it.

To update from an earlier version, unzip over the old folder, or load the new one and remove the old entry. Then press the reload arrow on `chrome://extensions`. An export in progress won't survive the swap: finish or stop it first.

Then [export your records](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.9.1/README.md#export-your-records), and [see what to ask your AI assistant](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#ask). The code isn't minified, so you can read exactly what runs.

## Before you start

- **Maccabi will text you.** Each export from the popup orders a fresh copy of your medical file, which sends an SMS and replaces the previous file on the site. It's the only change the extension makes to your account.
- **Keep the Maccabi Online tab in front** until the export finishes. Chrome pauses hidden tabs.
- **If your session ends,** log in again and press **Resume**. The files collected so far are kept.

## Privacy

- Talks only to `online.maccabi4u.co.il`. No servers, no analytics, no remote code.
- The extension's links open its website or its GitHub issue tracker in your own browser, and only when you click them. The extension sends neither one anything. The website's home page counts visits without cookies; the extension itself counts nothing.
- Never sees your password or one-time codes. It uses the session you're already logged in with.
- Apart from ordering the medical file, it only reads. Every request it makes is listed in [docs/endpoints/endpoints.json](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.9.1/docs/endpoints/endpoints.json).
- **The extension sends your records nowhere, but an AI service you open them in can read them.** Check what it keeps, and for how long, before you give it your health information.
- **The ZIP isn't encrypted.** It holds your health information: keep it somewhere safe, and take care when you share it.
