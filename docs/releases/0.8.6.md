# v0.8.6 — A progress bar that only moves forward

Two small fixes to what the popup shows at the end of an export, while your medical file is downloaded. Nothing about what is collected has changed.

> [!NOTE]
> Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Maccabi Healthcare Services.

## What's fixed

- **The progress bar never steps back.** When the wait for your medical file ended and its download began, the bar could drop before climbing again. Now it holds where it was until the download catches up.
- **The medical file's download is shown as a download.** Once Maccabi had the file ready, the popup still described the download of the PDF and of your letters list as the file being prepared. Now it is its own part of the export, as it already was in an export that ordered nothing.

The export's files and layout are the same as in 0.8.5, so an export paused on 0.8.5 resumes after the update.

## Install or update

You need Chrome 120 or newer.

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lmjcbhajlnbpldofejcglcdclceampjp)** and Chrome keeps it up to date — though store review can lag a day or two behind a release here.

To load this ZIP unpacked instead:

1. Download **health-records-export-for-maccabi-0.8.6.zip** from the assets below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder, the one with `manifest.json` in it. Keep that folder: Chrome loads the extension from it.

Updating from an earlier version: unzip over the old folder, or load the new one and remove the old entry, then press the reload arrow on `chrome://extensions`. An export in progress won't survive the swap — finish or stop it first.

Then [export your records](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.6/README.md#export-your-records), and [see what to ask your AI assistant](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#ask). The code isn't minified, so you can read exactly what runs.

## Before you start

- **Maccabi will text you.** Each export orders a fresh copy of your medical file, which sends an SMS and replaces the previous file on the site. It's the only change the extension makes to your account.
- **Keep the Maccabi Online tab in front** until the export finishes. Chrome pauses hidden tabs.
- **If your session ends,** log in again and press **Resume**. The files collected so far are kept.

## Privacy

- Talks only to `online.maccabi4u.co.il`. No servers, no analytics, no remote code.
- Never sees your password or one-time codes. It uses the session you're already logged in with.
- Apart from ordering the medical file, it only reads. Every request it makes is listed in [docs/endpoints.json](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.6/docs/endpoints.json).
- **The extension sends your records nowhere, but an AI service you open them in can read them.** Check what it keeps, and for how long, before you give it your health information.
- **The ZIP isn't encrypted.** It holds your health information: keep it somewhere safe, and take care when you share it.
