# v0.8.10 — A clearer popup

The popup now says what each step does before you take it, and each thing has one name, whether you see it in the popup, a notification or a message. Nothing about what is collected has changed.

> [!NOTE]
> Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Maccabi Healthcare Services.

## What's changed

- **Start says what it does.** Before you press it, the line under **Start** says it orders a fresh copy of your medical file, and that Maccabi will text you. On first use, amber marks only the step you can't undo: the new copy replaces the file on the site.
- **Every section, with its files.** While an export runs, the popup lists every section. Each one finished shows how many files it collected, under one *Files* heading. The reminder to keep the tab in front sits by the progress bar.
- **Each state is said once.** Ready, Paused, Stopped and Done no longer repeat their heading in a badge. When an export pauses or stops, **Resume** or **Try again** fills the row. **Cancel export** and **Delete collected files** are quieter buttons, and both still ask first.
- **One name for each thing.** It's "Export stopped" everywhere, including the badge and the notification, and "Export paused" for both kinds of pause. Every message tells you to resume the same way. Messages for an outdated run, a different member, or a missing login or tab are shorter, and say what to do next.
- **Open it in an AI** is marked as opening a web page.
- **The same ticks everywhere.** The ticks next to finished sections are drawn as images rather than typed characters, so they look the same on every computer.

## Policies

- **Privacy Policy** (effective 14 October 2026): the website's home page counts visits with GoatCounter, a cookieless image that sees your IP address and browser, like any web host. The pages inside the extension load no such image. The policy adds that an AI assistant you open the export in handles your files under its own privacy policy. There is now a private email address to write to.
- **Terms of Use** (effective 13 October 2026): anything an AI assistant explains or advises comes from that assistant, not from the extension or the developer. Check what it says against the record it names, and with your doctor. The terms add the same private email address.
- Both pages link to the extension's website.

These are not material changes, so you are not asked to accept again.

The export's files and layout are the same as in 0.8.9, so an export paused on 0.8.9 resumes after the update.

## Install or update

You need Chrome 120 or newer.

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lmjcbhajlnbpldofejcglcdclceampjp)** and Chrome keeps it up to date, though store review can lag a day or two behind a release here.

To load this ZIP unpacked instead:

1. Download **health-records-export-for-maccabi-0.8.10.zip** from the assets below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder, the one with `manifest.json` in it. Keep that folder: Chrome loads the extension from it.

To update from an earlier version, unzip over the old folder, or load the new one and remove the old entry. Then press the reload arrow on `chrome://extensions`. An export in progress won't survive the swap: finish or stop it first.

Then [export your records](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.10/README.md#export-your-records), and [see what to ask your AI assistant](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#ask). The code isn't minified, so you can read exactly what runs.

## Before you start

- **Maccabi will text you.** Each export from the popup orders a fresh copy of your medical file, which sends an SMS and replaces the previous file on the site. It's the only change the extension makes to your account.
- **Keep the Maccabi Online tab in front** until the export finishes. Chrome pauses hidden tabs.
- **If your session ends,** log in again and press **Resume**. The files collected so far are kept.

## Privacy

- Talks only to `online.maccabi4u.co.il`. No servers, no analytics, no remote code.
- The extension's links open its website or its GitHub issue tracker in your own browser, and only when you click them. The extension sends neither one anything. The website's home page counts visits without cookies; the extension itself counts nothing.
- Never sees your password or one-time codes. It uses the session you're already logged in with.
- Apart from ordering the medical file, it only reads. Every request it makes is listed in [docs/endpoints/endpoints.json](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.10/docs/endpoints/endpoints.json).
- **The extension sends your records nowhere, but an AI service you open them in can read them.** Check what it keeps, and for how long, before you give it your health information.
- **The ZIP isn't encrypted.** It holds your health information: keep it somewhere safe, and take care when you share it.
