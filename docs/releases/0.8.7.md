# v0.8.7 — A popup that says what to do next

A pass over the popup's wording and layout, from the first screen to the finished export. Nothing about what is collected has changed.

> [!NOTE]
> Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Maccabi Healthcare Services.

## What's new

- **Export ready leads to your AI assistant.** The finished export now pairs **Show in folder** with **How to ask an AI**, under the line that says what comes next. **Done** is now a quieter **Clear this summary**, which is what it does: the ZIP stays in your Downloads folder.
- **Problems are said in words.** An item that could not be exported now says what happened, for example that Maccabi Online no longer has the file, with the site's code kept beside it. The list scrolls inside the popup, and says that a new export tries those items again.
- **A stopped export tells you where to go.** An unexpected error is described plainly, with the technical detail set apart for a report and a **Report the problem** link beside it. When your session ends, the pause says what to do, without an HTTP code.

## What's fixed

- **Activity lines fit on one line.** Several were cut to an ellipsis beside a large count when Roboto isn't installed. They are shorter now.
- **The first file count names its unit.** The numbers beside finished sections were bare; the first one now reads as files.
- **The footer is easier to read and to click.** The version and the GitHub mark meet WCAG AA contrast, and every link is a 24px target.
- **Finding the icon.** The not-logged-in steps say the extension's icon is under Chrome's puzzle-piece button until you pin it.
- **The first-run notice is reordered.** What the extension does, including keeping you logged in to Maccabi while it runs, comes before what is asked of you.

## Policies

- The Terms of Use now link Maccabi's own terms of use and online services terms (effective 10 October 2026).
- The Privacy Policy names the renamed **Clear this summary** button (effective 12 October 2026).

Neither is a material change, so you are not asked to accept again.

The export's files and layout are the same as in 0.8.6, so an export paused on 0.8.6 resumes after the update.

## Install or update

You need Chrome 120 or newer.

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lmjcbhajlnbpldofejcglcdclceampjp)** and Chrome keeps it up to date — though store review can lag a day or two behind a release here.

To load this ZIP unpacked instead:

1. Download **health-records-export-for-maccabi-0.8.7.zip** from the assets below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder, the one with `manifest.json` in it. Keep that folder: Chrome loads the extension from it.

Updating from an earlier version: unzip over the old folder, or load the new one and remove the old entry, then press the reload arrow on `chrome://extensions`. An export in progress won't survive the swap — finish or stop it first.

Then [export your records](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.7/README.md#export-your-records), and [see what to ask your AI assistant](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#ask). The code isn't minified, so you can read exactly what runs.

## Before you start

- **Maccabi will text you.** Each export orders a fresh copy of your medical file, which sends an SMS and replaces the previous file on the site. It's the only change the extension makes to your account.
- **Keep the Maccabi Online tab in front** until the export finishes. Chrome pauses hidden tabs.
- **If your session ends,** log in again and press **Resume**. The files collected so far are kept.

## Privacy

- Talks only to `online.maccabi4u.co.il`. No servers, no analytics, no remote code.
- Never sees your password or one-time codes. It uses the session you're already logged in with.
- Apart from ordering the medical file, it only reads. Every request it makes is listed in [docs/endpoints.json](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.7/docs/endpoints.json).
- **The extension sends your records nowhere, but an AI service you open them in can read them.** Check what it keeps, and for how long, before you give it your health information.
- **The ZIP isn't encrypted.** It holds your health information: keep it somewhere safe, and take care when you share it.
