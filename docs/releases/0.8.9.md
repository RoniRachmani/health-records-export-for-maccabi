# v0.8.9 — One guide, on the website

How to open your export in an AI assistant is now part of [the extension's website](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/), and the popup opens it there. Nothing about what is collected has changed.

> [!NOTE]
> Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Maccabi Healthcare Services.

## What's changed

- **The guide opens on the website.** **See how**, **How to ask an AI** and the "Export ready" notification's button now open [Open it in Claude or ChatGPT](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#where) on the website, where the guide sits under the rest of the story: what to ask, how an export runs and what is in it. The page that shipped inside the extension is gone, so the guide now needs a connection.
- **While you wait, the nine questions.** The reminder shown while Maccabi prepares your medical file opens [what to ask](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#ask): nine questions, each answered with a picture. It still opens in a window of its own, so the Maccabi Online tab stays in front.
- **The guide says more than it did.** The first message is there to copy, beside why a folder works and an upload to a chat doesn't. A new section, **Built so you can check every answer**, shows what the export's `README.md` asks of the assistant, with an answer that names its file and page. The privacy section lists three settings to change in the assistant before it reads your records.
- **New exports point to the new address.** The line for you at the top of an export's `README.md` links to the guide on the website. The old address, `ai-assistant.html`, redirects there, so the link in an export you already have still works.

## Policies

- The Privacy Policy says which links the extension opens in your browser: the guide on its website, and its issue tracker on GitHub. Both are hosted by GitHub, which sees a visit like any web host does. The extension itself still talks only to Maccabi Online (effective 13 October 2026).

This is not a material change, so you are not asked to accept again.

The export's files and layout are the same as in 0.8.8, so an export paused on 0.8.8 resumes after the update.

## Install or update

You need Chrome 120 or newer.

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lmjcbhajlnbpldofejcglcdclceampjp)** and Chrome keeps it up to date — though store review can lag a day or two behind a release here.

To load this ZIP unpacked instead:

1. Download **health-records-export-for-maccabi-0.8.9.zip** from the assets below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder, the one with `manifest.json` in it. Keep that folder: Chrome loads the extension from it.

Updating from an earlier version: unzip over the old folder, or load the new one and remove the old entry, then press the reload arrow on `chrome://extensions`. An export in progress won't survive the swap — finish or stop it first.

Then [export your records](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.9/README.md#export-your-records), and [see what to ask your AI assistant](https://maccabi.ronirachmani.com/#ask). The code isn't minified, so you can read exactly what runs.

## Before you start

- **Maccabi will text you.** Each export from the popup orders a fresh copy of your medical file, which sends an SMS and replaces the previous file on the site. It's the only change the extension makes to your account.
- **Keep the Maccabi Online tab in front** until the export finishes. Chrome pauses hidden tabs.
- **If your session ends,** log in again and press **Resume**. The files collected so far are kept.

## Privacy

- Talks only to `online.maccabi4u.co.il`. No servers, no analytics, no remote code.
- The links to the guide open the extension's website in your own browser, when you click them. The extension sends the site nothing.
- Never sees your password or one-time codes. It uses the session you're already logged in with.
- Apart from ordering the medical file, it only reads. Every request it makes is listed in [docs/endpoints.json](https://github.com/RoniRachmani/health-records-export-for-maccabi/blob/v0.8.9/docs/endpoints.json).
- **The extension sends your records nowhere, but an AI service you open them in can read them.** Check what it keeps, and for how long, before you give it your health information.
- **The ZIP isn't encrypted.** It holds your health information: keep it somewhere safe, and take care when you share it.
