3. The medical file order

Each export orders a fresh copy of your full medical file through Maccabi Online. The extension sends the same request the site's own order button sends, but asks for your whole history, which is a wider date range than the site's form offers. By starting an export, you ask the extension to place that order for you. Maccabi sends you an SMS about it, and the new file replaces the previous one on the site. When a copy ordered earlier the same day over the same range is already waiting, the extension uses that one and orders nothing.

4. Not medical advice, and not a complete record

The extension copies what Maccabi Online provides and does not interpret it. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. An export may be incomplete or out of date: the site does not offer every record (for example, imaging studies, or visits older than 12 months), and some items can fail to download. Items that could not be exported are listed in the extension's popup when the export finishes. For medical decisions and official copies of your records, rely on Maccabi and your healthcare providers.
