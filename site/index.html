One click, and it collects everything

Maccabi Online shows your records one page at a time, and only so far back. The extension goes through all of it for you.

  1. Log in to Maccabi Onlineas usual, in Chrome.
  2. Press Start exportMaccabi texts you: it orders a fresh copy of your full medical file.
  3. Leave the tab openUsually a few minutes, up to 20. The ZIP lands in your Downloads.

Every record, every PDF, in one folder that's yours

Your full medical file on top, then a folder for each part of Maccabi Online: results, visits, prescriptions, referrals, vaccinations, letters.

Take it to a private doctor or one abroad. Keep it if you move or switch health funds. It's yours forever.

Not included: imaging studies, and visit summaries older than 12 months, which the site doesn't keep. See every folder
